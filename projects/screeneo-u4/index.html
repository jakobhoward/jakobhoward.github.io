Love it! Easy to use, connect to wifi and Bluetooth, great quality!! Highly recommend!! I have been projecting on a white wall inside and a portable screen outdoor. Awesome movie night!

The pre-launch list

The list that funded the opening hours

I wrote the pre-launch page to build a list of early adopters, enticed by early-bird pricing.

Below: the campaign page itself, annotated.

Cinematic immersion with vibrant colors and deep contrast wherever you are. Ultra Short Throw projection means room size doesn't matter.

Bring the theater home.

Connect your laptop and use it as a second screen.

Hook up your favorite consoles to play in True Full HD.

Watch in True Full HD 1080p with 60fps high-definition sharpness, full of the fine detail that can be lost at lower resolutions.

Featuring True Full HD, HDR10 and a cinema tuning with REC.709, colors remain lifelike with outstanding full high definition and clarity.

We made it incredibly easy to plug & play with your favorite devices right out of the box.

Use the 2x HDMI 2.1 and a USB-A to connect all your devices. Your gaming consoles, Apple TV, Roku Sticks, Amazon Fire TV, Chromecast, or Satellite receivers connect to the HDMI ports just like a TV without an extra adapter.

When not projecting your favorite movie, TV show, or game, Screeneo U4 can be used as a Bluetooth speaker. Pair your smartphone, laptop, or tablet to enjoy crisp tunes even when the projector is off.

Pair multiple audio devices. Set up a pair of speakers, or watch a movie privately with a friend on two sets of earphones, each with independent volume control.

Screeneo U4 is full dark matte for simplicity and modernity. Its compact design is meant to luxuriously and seamlessly blend into your home.

Our LED bulbs are rated to last 30,000 hours. That's over 40 years of usage if you watched one movie per day.

Opening Positioning

Instead of the tech, I lead with what it makes possible.

Counters the rebuttal that a projector is also a hassle.

Kills the #1 objection for projectors: room size.

The Lead Use Case

Theater leads because it’s the fantasy that sells a projector.

A Second Kind of Buyer

Same product, framed as work equipment — something a buyer can expense.

Familiarity

Leads on plug and play, so “will it work with my setup?” is answered above the port list.

A Second Job

A projector is idle most of the week. Filling those dead hours stretches one purchase past movie night.

Tech-Speak Translation

Makes an opaque spec actually mean something to humans. 40 years of use at 1 movie/day is far more tangible than the 30,000 hour rating.
