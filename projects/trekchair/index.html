Indiegogo Campaign Page

What I wrote and why.

View the live campaign on Indiegogo ↗
Rejecting the Compromise

Ultralight backpackers assume every ounce saved costs comfort. The title rejects that.

The system, in one clip

A pole that becomes a chair frame is hard to picture from a description — the clip shows both halves working as one system.

The weight claim

Comfort is hard to sell in the abstract, so I led with a figure a backpacker can weigh: 50% lighter than chairs its size.

Answer 'What Is It?' Early

A pole-and-chair hybrid is confusing on sight, so the equation comes early: frame + poles = chair.

Skimmers leave with the whole story; the curious get the proof underneath.

Make the Physics Believable

A 1.5 lb chair rated to 250 lb sounds too good to be true, so the copy pauses to name the materials.

Cork grip, carbon shaft, flip-lock: named so a backpacker can compare them with the poles they own.

The Emotional Peak

The headline promises a magic trick; the section that follows performs it!

Sells the moment itself: gear you forgot you were carrying turns into a place to sit, exactly when you want it.

The poles make their own case

Premium carbon fiber, argued on its own merits, so pole-owning backpackers still see value in the bundle.

The founder’s résumé

The founder section makes its case with a résumé — races run, trails completed. I wrote the messaging around that evidence.

The Smartwater Test

The Smartwater-bottle comparison answers the packability question with an object every hiker knows.
