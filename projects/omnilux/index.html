My Role Senior Copywriter at
Client

Wrote two Omnilux landing pages: a B2B pitch to clinicians, and the launch page for Omnilux Men.

Deliverables
Provider Landing Page Omnilux Men Landing Page Facebook & Instagram Ads

Approach

Two pages, two readers

Medical-grade light therapy

Omnilux builds award-winning LED light therapy devices for both medical and aesthetic practitioners.

…Sold for the home

The objective: turn the practices that use these devices in office into resellers of the at-home line.

A sales channel that didn’t exist before

The provider audience grew into 8% of Omnilux’s total email marketing list.

Skincare for men who skip it

For men with no skincare routine, I opened on one step and the complaints they already have, razor burn first. Omnilux later ran that line nearly word for word in an ad.

Provider Landing Page

What I wrote and why.

Business-to-business

The page recruits clinics to offer a new service. The device is the means.

This section leans on institutional credibility: four university medical centers a clinician already trusts.

The business case

The headline sells revenue to the provider, then results to their clients. Bullets aim at the practice’s bottom line.

The Add-On Pitch

Microneedling, IPL, lasers, fillers, Botox, topicals: I named the treatments a clinic already sells and positioned the mask as an add-on to each.

Proof From a Peer

Clinics take a working esthetician’s word over a manufacturer’s, so the proof here is a peer on video.

The paragraph under it names the specific conditions she treats, so a provider can match it against their own client list.

Make It Feel Earned

The close is an application, not a checkout.

Omnilux Men Landing Page

What I wrote and why.

Open on effort

I opened on one step because these men don’t keep a skincare routine.

Why “Engineered”

I chose “engineered” because it makes a skincare mask read as serious, practical technology.

Start with the razor

This paragraph lists the complaints men already have, starting with the one their razor causes.

A reason for a men’s version

“Thicker skin” gives a man a reason this mask is his, and the wavelength spec ties it to the hardware.

Close on the time cost

The close answers time, the objection most likely to stop a first-time buyer: results in 10 minutes.
