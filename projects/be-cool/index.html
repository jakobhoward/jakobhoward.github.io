The feeling had to be written

Nobody can press the foam through a screen, so the writing has to do the feeling. And since A+ modules can’t be skipped, the order matters as much as the words.

Below: the page itself, annotated module by module.

What I wrote and why.

The storefront banner

I led with the outcome. A shopper knows what this collection solves before scrolling.

Invisible Tech Made Visible

The images carry it here: the cooling layer peeled back, a hand pressing into it, then two thermal handprints — cool with it, hot without. The copy needs only summarize and reinforce.

Naming the Buyer

Naming the buyer out loud costs one line. The thermometers hand a skimmer the dual benefit without a sentence.

The phase change

The diagram walks the material from solid to liquid and back. A cycle that resets itself answers the durability question.

Sell Once, Apply Everywhere

After the sell is done, the next module offers the same cooling upgrade across the brand’s bestsellers, one button away.
