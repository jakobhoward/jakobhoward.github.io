Social posts: copy plus the mockup

I wrote each social post’s copy and built its mid‑fi mockup myself, so every concept arrived as a finished post.

Here’s the pre-launch page.

At just 1kg (2.5 lbs), Screeneo UL5 is a packable 100” cinema that you can take anywhere.

Since the Screeneo UL5 is placed right by the wall, there’s no need to worry about blocked images when someone walks by, and you don’t need to set aside space for the projection beam. So unless you’re a huge fan of shadow puppets, a short throw projector simply makes the most sense for most people.

Pre-Launch Landing Page

What I wrote and why.

The Ask Is an Email

The band runs three times down the page. Its progress bar shows the discount mostly spoken for, so the urgency points at the sign-up.

The Spec That Stops the Scroll

The 51 cm and 100″ diagram makes an impossible-sounding headline checkable.

Sold Against a Television

The money would otherwise go to a television, so the page names the TV as the thing to beat.

Earning Its Length

I tried to earn the attention I was asking for. The weight becomes a packable cinema, and a second benefit lands before the eye leaves.

Stood on End

Stand it upright and the same box becomes a monitor — a feature that changes what the product is rather than how well it does the job.

20 Years of Receipts

The timeline lays out seven earlier Screeneos, so a pre-launch ask for an email comes with a record behind it.

Common Sense as a Feature

A projector by the wall means nobody walks through the beam. I turned a placement fact into a benefit, and a shadow-puppet joke retires the objection.

The Off State, Sold

At rest there is nothing on the wall, and the room comes back until showtime.
