Indiegogo Campaign Page

What I wrote and why.

View the live campaign on Indiegogo ↗
Selling the Movie Night

The opening sells the off state as hard as the movie night: a huge screen only at showtime, a blank wall the rest of the week — where a TV leaves its black rectangle up all day.

Nine Answers Up Front

It front-loads every spec a buyer might otherwise google, before the page expands the biggest ones.

Throw Ratio, Given a Section

Throw ratio is the spec this category competes on, so it gets its own section, with a diagram and size table I conceptualized.

A Visual I Sketched First

The 130% color-gamut lockup was my concept — a wheel that makes an abstract spec visible, with the standards badged beneath it.

Reviews Before There Are Reviews

There are no customer reviews to show at launch, so I styled press quotes the way I’d style reviews — outside voices vouching for the specs buyers doubt.

A Century of Philips

Crowdfunding’s real objection is whether the thing ever ships. The copy stakes a century of Philips and a decade of projectors against that doubt.

The 1080p-vs-4K Frame

One frame carries two claims. The 4K half is sharper and its color is richer, so the DLP color-accuracy line above it is proved by the same picture.

Copy That Ran Twice

This section’s daylight argument also ran as an Instagram caption before launch, in the social posts further down this page.

Show the Difference

Most theaters weren’t even projecting 4K yet — ‘Shown in 1080p’ quietly says the movie looks better at home. The Batman was in cinemas at the time; that’s why it’s the stake.

The Hours It’s Switched Off

The design section answers the objection no spec sheet can: the headline promises a projector you barely notice until it’s powered on.

A Second Job for the Wall

Video calls at wall scale give the projector a weekday.

Placed Against Their Own Cheaper Model

The upsell has to clear the gap to Philips’ own U4, so the trade is shown side by side. Naming what the cheaper model gives up makes the premium read as measured.
