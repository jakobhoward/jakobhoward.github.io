Pre-Launch Landing Page

What I wrote and why.

One Screen to Earn an Email

The headline spends the screen on recharge speed and solar, and the bullets preview three of the sections below.

Demand as the Opening Argument

The first card opens on a shortage, demand outpacing supply, so the scarcity comes from the market instead of a campaign clock.

Two Orange Words

I colored Power and Move so anyone skimming catches the “power move.” I still like the copy under it, which turns a capacity figure into a home you can take anywhere.

Handle and Weight, Labeled

I suggested labeling the handle and the weight right on the photo, so On The Go shows how you’d carry it and how heavy it is.

Puns on the Press Row

I couldn’t resist two jokes on a battery’s press row. The reviews spark, and the subhead talks up the energy.

Runtimes, Not Watt-Hours

1000Wh is a spec. The grid restates it in the reader’s own appliances, from 75 phone charges to 14 hours of mini fridge.

The Caveat in Plain Sight

The 2-hour claim carries its own fine print — AC or gas generator only.

Six Hours From the Sun

The outage fear and its off-grid answer share one band.

Trust, Photographed

The quiet claim could have been decibels. Instead it’s a man asleep on a nebulizer running off the battery, where noise and reliability both have to be true.

Both Buyers, Named Once

One overlay names the two people the page has been alternating between.

Three Unnamed Columns

The comparison names no competitor: three gray columns against the orange one, so the table argues value without starting a fight.

Even the Specs Get a Laugh

A spec sheet is all plain numbers, so I hid a little wordplay in its heading. Read the second word as spec-tacular.

Steel, Numbered

Three numbered renders show the galvanized frame, the two-layer case, and the ventilation gaps, so each durability claim has a picture.

A Famous Line, Rewired

I borrowed the line for the close and swapped its last word. You expect responsibility, and you get savings.
