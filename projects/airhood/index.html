Pre-Launch Landing Page

What I wrote and why.

View the live campaign on Kickstarter ↗
Three Enemies

The hero skips features. It leads with three problems every kitchen already has, and the product enters as the fix.

Explaining Before Selling

An icon grid this early isn't how I usually open. The page had to say what the thing even is before it could argue anything.

Two Filters, Two Jobs

Each filter gets its own claim and its own photo: charcoal for the air, oil for the surfaces. Splitting them doubles the perceived engineering in a device the size of a kettle.

Corded or Cordless

Two models, side by side: cord for full power, battery for 180 minutes. A first-time product can't send the buyer off to a comparison chart.

Situations Over Specs

Portability is an abstraction until the reader can say where. The section answers with places a mounted hood can't follow.

From Convenience to Health

Grease, smoke, and filters come first. The page closes its argument on the reader's health — the line I carried onto the campaign page.

Everything Funnels to an Email

The page never shows a price or a buy button. Its one conversion is an address.
