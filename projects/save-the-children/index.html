When the schools closed

30 million US kids relied on school for lunch, and once COVID hit, many had to go without. On a platform normally used for early adopters to pre-order the latest tech gadgets, this campaign asked for donations to feed children in need.

I delivered
  • Paid social ad copy
  • Influencer outreach

Influencers, one DM at a time

With no budget to pay them, I pitched cold. Each note told one person why this campaign, and why now.
