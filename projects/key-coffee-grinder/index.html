Indiegogo Campaign Page

What I wrote and why.

Fewer-Steps Reframe

Single-dosing has a reputation for fuss, so I led on what disappears from the routine.

The Grind Ladder

This visual was my concept — ‘consistent grind’ made visible, coarse to fine, each rung tied to its brewer.

The Homework, Cashed In

Oil and old grounds left in the burrs carry one bean’s flavor into the next. I had to learn that myself, then built this section around it.

Scannable Spec Grid

I compressed six proof points into an icon grid.

A Technique, Named and Built In

I explained the tumbler’s job with the technique’s proper name, WDT, a step baristas do by hand — so an expert reader knows the machine knows the ritual.

Heirloom Quality

Spread over decades, $1,649 is a different number. I paired that longevity with the materials story, so buying once also reads as the responsible choice.

The Person Behind It

I drafted the founder messaging that puts a person behind the machine.

I wrote this section about Weber Workshops itself.
