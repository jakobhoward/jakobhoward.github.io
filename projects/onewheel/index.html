Pint X Product Page

What I wrote and why.

Small, Framed as an Asset

The size objection gets answered in the tagline, before it can form. Sandwiching the small-sounding word — 'mini' — between two power words makes compactness read as a feature.

Range Takes the Lead Slot

Range is the Pint X’s biggest upgrade over the Pint. The number makes the case; the short second sentence gives it soul.

Speed, Reframed

Boxing's weight-class logic turns 18 mph into a win for the board's size.

Spec → Feeling

The promise comes first, so the mileage that follows reads as proof of something already sold.

Measured, Then Sold

The tip-to-tip number proves it fits into a commute; the slang that follows sells how it feels to own.

Reassurance First

Every first-timer is silently asking the same thing: what if I can’t stop? The plain-words promise answers first; the named tech backs it up.

Feature Named in Slang

Peak 2021, I know. The wink fit the moment and the audience.

Testimonial as Punctuation

The strongest thing the page can promise is habit — and that claim only lands in a customer’s voice.

Price Reframed as Monthly

The headline gives the monthly figure the display type, then the pair underneath sets full price and monthly at the same size. An open comparison, nothing to feel tricked by later.

onewheel.com/products/onewheel-gt

GT Product Page

What I wrote and why.

GT Hero and Stat Bar

'Quantum leap' is a claim, so it needs backup: top speed, range, and price. Three objective numbers instill confidence before the Buy Now button.

Written in Rider Slang

'Ready to go full send?' opens in rider slang, then 'Carvy? Grippy? Choose two.' only lands if you know the cliche it breaks.

A Rhyme for the Handle

A carry handle is the least glamorous feature on the page, so the header rhymes: it promises the ride first, then lets the body copy do the snaps-in-place housekeeping.

Financing Does the Math

The priciest board in the line closes on the payment-sized number: $92 a month sits beside the $2,200 it divides, at the same size.
