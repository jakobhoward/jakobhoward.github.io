Pre-Launch Landing Page

What I wrote and why.

Naming the Collision

I let the headline say the clumsy compound out loud. The line beneath fuses the halves into one product.

What’s Already in the Bag

The tripod you own is never with you. Your water bottle is, so I built the opening argument on that gap.

Written to Be Scanned

I kept every label to two or three words so the grid reads at a glance.

From Feature to Instruction

The grid lists the magnets. Here the headline tells you what to do with them.

A Physics Claim, Photographed

The feature grid drew the counter-balance as a diagram. Here a phone tilts on the lid in a kitchen, so the angle promise is shown in use.

The Dog Bowl Gets Its Section

The hero only hints at the dog bowl. This is where owners get the full pitch. Everyone else has already been sold.

Answering the Excluded Phone

MagSafe quietly disqualifies most Android phones and every older iPhone. The page answers that in copy, so an excluded reader learns they’re covered without hunting.
