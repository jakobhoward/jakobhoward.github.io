One bought, one given

Occa launched on a promise bigger than the product: every unit bought funds a solar light in off-grid Kenya or South Africa.

I delivered
  • Campaign page copy

Occa Solar Light comes with a detachable metal stand and a comfortable headband to allow you to have light in any situation. Convert your Occa from a posable lamp to a portable light, for reading, pitching your tent or simply walking your dog in the dark.

Occa’s high efficiency PV solar panel gives you up to 15 hours of light from a single charge. There’s backup USB-C charging too, so you’ll never be left in the dark - even on the cloudiest of days.

We pledge that for every Occa Solar light purchased, we will donate one of our award-winning SM100 solar lights. When you buy a light, you’re directly helping give people without access to electricity in Kenya and South Africa a solar light to read, study and learn safely after dark.

It’s not just about making life easier for our fellow humans. We also aim to make a difference for other species.

Our solar lights are used to help with conservation and the 24-hour protection of native, and endangered animals.

Choose between cool and warm light settings.

Help the planet by reducing waste and toxic chemicals from disposable batteries.

Inbuilt solar PV panel means your light comes straight from the sun - not expensive, disposable batteries. Charge Occa over and over again and never pay to power your light!

Our product is designed to be truly sustainable, with modular features that can be replaced so it won’t end up in landfill after a season.

By changing the way people study, work, and play, we can fight climate change together with sustainable lights that work for people and the planet.

We want to bring everyone, everywhere - access to clean, modern energy by connecting people to the sun. We deliver end-to-end, scalable solar solutions that save money and combat climate change.

We work in communities with limited access to electricity, empowering them to eliminate carbon emissions and helping them save money by alleviating the cost of kerosene fuel.

Qhubeka is a charity that helps people with little access to transportation in Africa. Their bicycles improve access to schools, clinics and jobs, and make it easier and quicker to carry goods, like water and food. As the recipients of our solar lights, Qhubeka can make traveling by bicycle safer and brighter.

HODI works to champion justice and development in the Horn of Africa through advocacy, education, peace building and sustainable livelihoods. Our lights will be distributed to the young women and girls they work with. This will give them access to a lightweight, portable solar light for studying, reading and traveling safely without having to rely on kerosene fuel or pay for expensive batteries.

Get Ahead Project Schools are low-fee, independent schools situated in Komani and Whittlesea, Eastern Cape, South Africa, providing quality education to black African youth, from local rural townships. Our solar lights will be donated to students to ensure that they have safe, solar lighting for studying when electricity fails, due to lack of reliable and consistent service provision.

Indiegogo Campaign Page

What I wrote and why.

Two Tools in One Frame

I opened on the Occa doing both of its jobs, a lamp on its stand and a head torch on its band. One light that does both is easier to buy than a niche gadget.

Sun or USB-C

Six hours of sun, three of USB-C — charging turned into a schedule a camper can plan around.

The Give Is the Sell

Nobody bought an Occa only for themselves, so I put the pledge high on the page.

What a Light Buys

Six icons show how far the impact of a gifted light stretches.

A Recipient, Quoted

The give side gets its own testimonial: a Kenyan Masai woman on never having to look for power.

The Upsell Is a Donation

Each step up adds lights for someone else and none for the backer.

Honest Tradeoffs

Three brightness tiers are priced in hours: brighter costs battery, and the page says how much.

One Object, Five Verbs

The versatility section is written as five verbs, each a scene the reader can picture without a spec. The card also admits who the features were really designed for.

Vouched For at Both Ends

Each quote carries a place: James in Kenya, Mondli and Henry in London. Both ends of the buy-one-give-one deal vouch for Namene’s lights.

Health, Money, Energy

The donation’s effect is split into three ledgers a skeptical reader can audit separately.

On the Ground

The give-one promise names its partners, each with what the lights will do there, starting with Qhubeka’s riders.
