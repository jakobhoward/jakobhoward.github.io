What I wrote and why.

The 2-in-1

The 2-in-1 lockup was my concept: an earphone and a speaker glyph sit under the number, the premise landing as a little equation (well, technically an expression).

Two products land in one sentence: categories bold, grammar light — the nouns read first.

The moving render was my call. LUA was hard to describe in a line but clicks the moment you watch the buds and case become one object.

Specs Written as Sensation

The spec is translated into something an ear can picture — a drum hit. The driver size sits underneath.

The Survey's Loudest Worry

Battery was the pre-launch survey's loudest worry — a speaker and earphones in one compact case invited doubt — so 40 hours is on the page twice.

The Social Argument

The 2-in-1 gets its emotional case: the point of a speaker in your pocket is other people.

Credibility Before Checkout

Three named faces with titles show there are real people behind the product.

A recording artist's ear on the tuning is a claim no spec sheet can make.
